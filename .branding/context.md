# FindMySpare: Brand Brief (compact)

**Scope:** `brand-identity` · **Mode:** `brand-evolution` (name is final) · **Primary domain:** technology · **Overlay:** b2b (garages, suppliers, fleets buy/approve)

## Confirmed (from the user and the codebase)
- Name stays **FindMySpare**. User: "keep the name Find My Spare".
- Product: an India auto-parts marketplace (catalog + cart + checkout), a request → multi-seller quote engine, **Garage OS** (job cards, estimates, GST invoices, payment collection, invoice verification) and a **Vehicle Passport** (lifetime service and parts record per vehicle).
- Users: car and bike owners; mechanics and independent garages (the main buyers); parts traders and distributors (sellers); later fleets and insurers.
- The prototype is **web only**. Payments are mocked.
- Feel reference: **Swiggy**. The user said "very clean UI". Read as: white canvas, one confident warm colour, chunky friendly sans, big rounded cards, photo- and icon-led browsing, generous spacing, fast and playful micro-interactions.
- Research (docs/strategy): the top pains are wrong fitment, fake parts, opaque prices, slow sourcing and bad returns. Trust is the category's missing ingredient.

## Assumptions (labelled; change any of them)
- A1: Most usage is on budget Android phones, often outdoors. This needs high contrast and big tap targets.
- A2: Hindi/Devanagari is needed soon, so the type system must pair with a Devanagari face.
- A3: The colour must not copy Swiggy orange (#FC8019). We borrow the *feel*, not the *trade dress*.
- A4: Sub-brands (Garage, Passport, Verified) ride the master brand. There is no separate logo per product.

## Unknowns (not blocking)
- Trademark status of the final mark. Preliminary only; needs legal counsel.
- The final domain and app-store presence.

## Foundation
- **Positioning:** For Indian vehicle owners and the mechanics who fix their vehicles, who waste days hunting for parts they can't verify, FindMySpare is the parts marketplace and garage network that finds the right part, from a verified seller, and remembers everything fitted to your vehicle. It does this through multi-seller quotes, fitment confirmed by real jobs, and a verifiable service record. Unlike catalog sites and market runners, it guarantees fit and keeps the proof.
- **Transformation:** from "running around Kashmere Gate hoping it fits" to "found, fitted, recorded".
- **Brand role:** the dependable *finder* and *keeper of proof* for the Indian aftermarket.
- **Core promise:** *Sahi part. Pakka record.* (The right part. A permanent record.)
- **Reasons to believe:** GSTIN-verified sellers, competing quotes, confirmed-fit data, QR-verifiable invoices, Vehicle Passport history.
- **Personality tensions:** mechanically credible but *not greasy*; friendly like a consumer app but *not toy-like*; fast but *not careless*; Indian-street-smart but *not cluttered*.
- **Anti-positioning:** not a dark luxury "premium auto" brand; not a cold enterprise-blue B2B portal; not a discount bazaar of red banners.
- **Emotional benefit:** relief and confidence ("it'll fit, and I can prove it").
- **Tone of voice:** short, warm, Hinglish-friendly, concrete numbers, no jargon.
- **Visual territories:**
  1. *The Find:* lens, pin, spotlight. Search made instant.
  2. *The Part:* hex nut, bolt, socket. Honest mechanical geometry.
  3. *The Record:* stamp, tick, card. Proof you can keep.
