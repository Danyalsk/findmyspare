# Nouns and concept catalog

## Nouns generated (24)
**Objects:** hex nut, bolt, socket wrench, spanner, spark plug, piston, gear, tyre, headlight.
**Structures:** location pin, map grid, shelf/rack, crate.
**Movements:** search sweep, delivery arrow, click-into-place.
**Proof:** tick, stamp/seal, QR, card/passport, receipt.
**People/roles:** mechanic's hand, magnifier lens.
**Cultural:** number plate (India HSRP).

## Approved (15) and rationale
hex nut · bolt · spanner · socket · location pin · magnifier lens · tick · stamp/seal · card/passport · receipt · number plate · click-into-place · delivery arrow · shelf · spark plug.
These map to the three territories: Find (lens, pin, arrow), Part (nut, bolt, spanner, socket, spark plug), Record (tick, seal, card, receipt, plate).

## Rejected nouns
- **gear:** generic "engineering/settings" shorthand, low ownability.
- **piston, tyre, headlight:** car-only; excludes bikes and EVs.
- **QR:** a mechanism, not a symbol; it dates fast.
- **crate:** reads "logistics company".
- **mechanic's hand:** too detailed at small sizes.
- **map grid:** too thin and fussy.
