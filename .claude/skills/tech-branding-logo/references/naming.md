# Naming module

## Territories

Derive 3–6 territories from strategy, such as outcome, metaphor, motion, trust/control, human role, technical mechanism, or future expansion. Generate across descriptive, suggestive, metaphorical, invented, compound, technical, human/character, and abstract mechanisms.

## Candidate schema

For each candidate record: `name`, `mechanism`, `pronunciation`, `meaning/association`, `strategic fit`, `distinctiveness hypothesis`, `future flexibility`, `tone`, `risks`, `descriptor option`, and `screening status`.

## Screening

Check spelling, pronunciation, unintended meanings, category crowding, obvious competitor resemblance, search ambiguity, and preliminary domain/app-store/social/trademark signals when tools are available. Report source, date, geography, and uncertainty. Preliminary screening is not legal clearance; recommend professional counsel before adoption.

## Selection

Shortlist 5–10 candidates, explain tradeoffs, then recommend one with a rationale that does not depend on an invented backstory. If a user says the name is final, classify fit and continue unless the conflict is material.
