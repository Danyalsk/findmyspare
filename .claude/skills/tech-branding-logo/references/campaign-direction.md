# Campaign direction

## Brief and continuity

Capture objective, audience, product/offer, occasion, channels, deliverables, duration, parent brand, references and fixed requirements. Reuse known answers. Exact product appearance, logo treatment, approved copy/claims/disclosures and attribution may be fixed; never invent missing commercial facts.

Infer freedom from the request:
- `brand-led`: retain identity prominently; vary message and campaign idea.
- `brand-adjacent`: vary colour, type, imagery or tone while retaining explicitly named recognition cues.
- `independent`: build a different visual world while preserving specified attribution and fixed requirements.

Record permitted overrides, immutable elements, application boundaries and duration in a campaign-specific manifest before execution. An adjacent/independent setting is not permission to change master brand files. Missing parent assets may permit a labelled concept, but prevent claiming verified brand fidelity.

## Make the campaign

1. Develop brief-linked ideas with audience action, message, visual mechanism and rationale. Preserve rejected directions.
2. Select a direction; define composition, typography treatment, photo/illustration style, materials, palette and copy hierarchy. A moodboard is supporting evidence, not the finished concept.
3. Produce a key visual. Generation instructions specify subject, exact product constraints, composition, reserved copy areas, treatment, aspect ratio and prohibited changes. Use supplied logos and exact typography in editable composition where generated rendering cannot preserve them.
4. Define variation rules and create requested channel assets: adapt hierarchy and composition, not just automatic crops. Record dimensions, safe regions and copy per channel; verify current platform specifications only when necessary for production.
5. Inspect product fidelity, text, claims, attribution, readability and continuity. Deliver key visual, requested assets, editable sources where possible, variation rules and QC. Label missing production files explicitly.

Example: a monochrome apparel brand requests a loud nostalgic summer drop for students. Load fashion and this workflow, establish recognisable elements, and develop lettering, photography, colour and layouts inside that boundary. Do not send it through renaming or permanent logo redesign.
