# Packaging production overlay

Load only when packaging is a deliverable. Establish pack form, dimensions/dieline, substrate, print process, variants, sales channel and supplied mandatory copy as these become necessary. For early concepts, label assumed dimensions and missing production inputs.

Prioritise brand, product/category, variant and supporting information based on purchase context. Test shelf or thumbnail recognition and variant distinction without relying solely on colour. Inspect front/back/side hierarchy, folds, seams, bleed, safe areas and reproduction limits against supplier specifications. Do not invent regulatory copy or claim compliance; unresolved mandatory copy blocks production release.

Deliver editable flat artwork when possible; a perspective mockup communicates appearance but is not a print-ready dieline. Use approved product imagery and exact supplied text. Record unknown printer requirements and distinguish concept approval from production readiness. Original guidance; book research pending.
