# B2B buying-context overlay

Load when organisations purchase, approve or procure the offer. Keep the underlying industry module: a food wholesaler and enterprise software company do not share one visual style.

Understand users, champions, economic buyers, procurement and risk owners only where relevant. Ask what evidence helps each buyer act: specifications, samples, service reliability, case studies or implementation support. Translate verified proof into clear hierarchy and reusable sales materials. Do not invent logos of customers, testimonials, certifications or performance claims.

Compare an expressive category-led system with a more restrained information-led system against the actual buying journey. Avoid using corporate blue as a substitute for credibility. Test information findability, evidence quality, buyer-role relevance and consistency across sales and operational touchpoints. Return assumptions and conflicts using the specialist contract.
