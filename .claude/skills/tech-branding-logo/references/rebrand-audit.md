# Rebrand audit

## Audit dimensions

Review current mark, wordmark, app icon, colors, typography, UI expression, motion, documentation, social assets, and customer-facing templates for: recognition, distinctiveness, product/category fit, accessibility, legibility, scalability, technical production, emotional fit, and competitive separation.

## Equity inventory

For each asset classify: `retain` (valuable and functional), `evolve` (valuable but weak or inconsistent), or `retire` (misleading, generic, inaccessible, obsolete, or unusable). Record evidence and confidence. Never erase recognition merely for novelty.

## Change scope

- **Refresh:** stable strategy; refine type, spacing, color, accessibility, icon, and rules.
- **Evolution:** product or audience has grown; preserve one or two recognizable cues and rebuild the system.
- **Reset:** current identity is harmful, legally unusable, tied to a discarded name, or fundamentally misleading. Still seek continuity through rollout, naming, motion, or selected cues.

## Migration plan

Sequence old/new coexistence, product release, website/docs, app stores, repositories/package registries, customer communication, partner assets, support content, internal templates, deprecation, and monitoring. Include owners, dependencies, rollback notes, and recognition/confusion signals.
