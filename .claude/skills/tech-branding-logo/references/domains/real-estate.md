# Real estate

Original working guidance; book validation pending. Use the specialist contract.

## Context that changes decisions

Is this a developer, brokerage, individual property or place? Establish location, buyer motivations, purchase stage, built versus planned status and parent/project relationships.

## Possibilities and tradeoffs

Place-derived lettering or patterns may create specificity; architectural structure may guide a system if genuinely tied to the site. Buyer confidence may require clear plans and evidence more than a luxury emblem.

## Clichés and failures

Generic roofs, skylines and gold crests; invented amenities, completion dates, investment returns or misleading renderings.

## Applications

Test hoardings and building signage at viewing distance, listing thumbnails, brochures, maps and wayfinding where requested. Preserve project/developer hierarchy and label conceptual renders.

## Evaluation

Place specificity, hierarchy, distance legibility, buyer relevance and consistency between claims and supplied facts.
