# Fashion and apparel

Original working guidance; book validation pending. Use the specialist contract.

## Context that changes decisions

Subculture, audience, price tier, materials, silhouettes, brand attitude, collection story and relationship between permanent brand and seasonal expression.

## Possibilities and tradeoffs

Wordmarks, custom lettering, monograms, emblems and illustrative identities can each work. Let garment placement and cultural context influence form. Editorial photography may carry a collection story without changing the master logo.

## Clichés and failures

Unexamined luxury imitation, generic streetwear distressing, borrowed cultural symbols without context, invented material or sustainability claims.

## Applications

Test labels, hangtags, ecommerce, garment print and embroidery at intended size with supplier limits. Preserve garment fit, colour and details in campaign imagery. Seasonal palettes belong to campaign records.

## Evaluation

Attitude and audience fit, garment feasibility, recognisable brand codes, image fidelity and coherent seasonal variation.
