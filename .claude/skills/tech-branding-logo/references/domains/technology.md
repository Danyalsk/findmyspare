# Technology

Original working guidance; book validation pending. Use the specialist contract.

## Context that changes decisions

Product category, user sophistication, workflow, trust needs and the role of a parent platform.

## Possibilities and tradeoffs

A restrained wordmark may support complex enterprise workflows; a distinct symbol can support repeated app recognition. Illustrative or expressive identity may fit creative tools. Tie choices to audience and use, not a mandatory geometric aesthetic.

## Clichés and failures

Automatic brains, sparks, circuits, shields, robots and gradients; unsupported security claims.

## Applications

For requested digital applications, test app icon, 16×16 favicon, browser extension, documentation, UI header, light/dark and monochrome. Test hardware marks at their actual reproduction scale.

## Evaluation

Recognition at the actual size; contrast and legibility; coherent UI/documentation extensions; credible product promise.
