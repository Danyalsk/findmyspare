# Food and hospitality

Original working guidance; book validation pending. Use the specialist contract.

## Context that changes decisions

Cuisine/category, eating or gifting occasion, price, service model, provenance, cultural context and retail versus delivery versus hotel supply.

## Possibilities and tradeoffs

Lettering can convey maker character, editorial typography can convey service experience, and product imagery can aid appetite and recognition. Select based on category and occasion; premium does not automatically mean minimal or gold.

## Clichés and failures

Generic chef hats, wheat stalks or seals with no product link; culturally inaccurate motifs; invented ingredients, certifications or provenance.

## Applications

Test menu reading, storefront distance, delivery thumbnails and food-image fidelity. If packaging is requested load the packaging overlay; distinguish retail impulse from hospitality service or wholesale supply.

## Evaluation

Category and variant recognition, appetite or service fit, price expectations, readable information hierarchy and believable provenance.
